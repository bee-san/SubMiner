## Exact scope

The covered work is any Affirmer-owned portion, present in this fork on the
effective date, that implements, tests, documents, styles, or configures the
immersion statistics dashboard and its supporting capture, API, daemon,
database, navigation, vocabulary, session, media, trend, or maintenance
behavior.

The audited path families are:

- `stats/**`;
- `src/**/*stat*`, including the stats daemon, word helper, preload, services,
  window runtime, CLI, config, and tests;
- `launcher/commands/stats-command.ts`;
- statistics-related `docs/**`, `docs-site/**`, and `backlog/**` records.

This path list locates the relevant feature. It does not dedicate another
author's work and does not turn an entire mixed-authorship file into CC0.

At audit baseline `da0087bba65433910a43812dd9cc02b053af0615`,
the commits touching these path families were recorded under
`sudacode <suda@sudacode.com>`, not under the known Affirmer identities.
Accordingly, this declaration makes no file-wide ownership claim over the
current SubMiner statistics implementation. It records the Affirmer's express
CC0 dedication for any qualifying statistics contribution they own, including
uncredited or metadata-rewritten contributions, while leaving every other
person's rights untouched.
